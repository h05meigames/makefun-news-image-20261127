An entry for the [news-image-20261127 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261127):
sum up 27 November's world news in one image.

## The image

A 1024×1024 newspaper front page for **THE WORLD — Friday 27 November 2026**,
drawn entirely in code with Pillow. No photograph, no stock art, no network
fetch: every headline, panel and illustration is generated by
`render_frontpage.py` when the program runs.

- **Lead — Drones kill two in Kuwait.** Two Italian Rheinmetall Italia
  technicians die when drones strike a Kuwaiti air-defence site. Kuwait and
  Italy blame Iran; Tehran denies any role and calls it a false flag. A
  schematic map of the Gulf marks the site.
- **Storm blacks out 900,000 as parade is postponed.** A coastal storm cuts
  power to about 900,000 homes and businesses on the US East Coast and forces
  the first-ever postponement of the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade,
  moved to Saturday. Gusts reached 65 mph.
- **Gaza.** Hamas weighs its response after Israeli strikes kill 10 in Gaza,
  including senior Hamas official Khaled Abu Nada and his wife, on a third
  night of bombing.
- **Bangkok.** An explosion at the Bangchak oil refinery injures at least
  nine people; the company monitors air quality.
- **Britain.** Net migration falls to its lowest since 1997, excluding the
  pandemic years.
- **By the numbers.** 2 killed in Kuwait · 900,000 without power in the US ·
  10 killed in Gaza · 9 hurt in Bangkok · 65 mph storm gusts.
- **Also today.** Gukesh and Sindarov draw Game 3 of the World Chess
  Championship · Kim Jong Un vows more nuclear weapons · US GDP revised up
  to 4.3% · a diesel tanker capsizes off Ibiza.

## How it runs

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22) executes `python3 render_frontpage.py`, which draws the
page deterministically — same bytes on every run — and copies the result to
`<output-dir>/entry.png`. The image is produced at runtime, so no PNG is
committed to this repository.
